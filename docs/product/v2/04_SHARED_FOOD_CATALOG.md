# V2 Shared Food Catalog

## Ownership

Food visibility is global. `created_by_principal_id`,
`updated_by_principal_id`, and `archived_by_principal_id` are audit provenance,
not an authorization scope. Only administrators may mutate the catalog.

Diary remains Principal-owned. Each Diary entry references one non-null global
`food_id` and stores only the historical Diary event state needed to reproduce
the amount consumed.

## Current Food truth

Food nutrition and other mutable Food catalog data are current authoritative
truth. Diary reads and totals calculate from the current Food record and the
entry's recorded consumed amount. Correcting Food nutrition therefore updates
past Diary calculations after refetch. No Food nutrition snapshot is stored.

Recorded Diary measurement state is historical. Changing a Food's default unit
or serving amount does not change an existing entry's consumed amount. A
referenced Food cannot change between mass and volume nutrition dimensions
without an authoritative conversion mechanism.

## Lifecycle

- Unarchived Foods appear in normal catalog, search, and picker results.
- Archived Foods remain resolvable for historical Diary calculations and admin views.
- Administrators may restore an archived Food.
- An unreferenced Food may be hard-deleted after confirmation.
- A Food referenced by Diary history is archived instead of hard-deleted.
- Diary-to-Food referential integrity uses `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

There is no Food status field. Archive state is represented only by
`archived_at` and `archived_by_principal_id`.

## Duplicate protection

Duplicate identity is global. `normalized_name` is generated by the backend,
and the persisted duplicate key protects concurrent writes without exposing the
normalized value as a user input.
